Is DOL deciding PERM cases faster than a year ago? The decisions chart now answers that at any scale. Pick days, weeks, months or years, and each column carries a mark for the same days a year earlier.

## What this chart shows

- Days: the last 30, each beside the same weekday 52 weeks before.
- Weeks: the last 26, Monday to Sunday.
- Months: the last 12. This is the month list that sat lower on [the decisions page](/perm-decision-activity), now with a chart above it.
- Years: every year on record, back to 2007.
- Beside the chart, the usual shape of what you're looking at. For days and weeks it's a typical week. For months and years it's a typical year, month by month.
- On [the decisions page](/perm-decision-activity), every day, week, month or year is listed under the chart with its change against a year earlier.

## How it's counted

Weeks, months and years are compared per working day, so a holiday or a month still running doesn't read as DOL slowing down. A stretch that neither DOL's published file nor our daily check covers is drawn as an empty frame, never as zero. As of November 17, 2026, that's July 1 to August 26, 2026. The weeks DOL was shut in October 2025 are named, never compared.

## Where it shows up

The same chart, with the same numbers, is on [the homepage](/), [the decisions page](/perm-decision-activity), [today's PERM update](/today) and [PERM statistics from DOL's own records](/perm-by-the-numbers). The 90-day and 180-day views on today's update are now the weeks view.
