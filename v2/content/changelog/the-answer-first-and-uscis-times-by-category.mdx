This release moves the answer and the case box to the top of the homepage. It also fills in USCIS's own posted times, one category at a time.

## The homepage opens with the answer

- The [homepage](/) now puts the month DOL is working on, with its chart, beside the case number box. On a phone the box comes right after the answer. Before, it sat a screen and a half down.
- Under the box, "Past PERM already?" links straight to [I-140 times](/uscis-processing-times/i-140), the [visa bulletin](/visa-bulletin) and [I-485 times](/uscis-processing-times/i-485).
- The four figures sit in one row under both. The count of wage requests waiting now opens the [prevailing wage queue calculator](/tools/pwd-calculator).
- The approval bar has a heading of its own: "PERM approval rate".
- On the road from PERM to a green card, the I-140 stop now prints the time within which USCIS decided half its I-140s in the newest quarter.

## USCIS's posted time, by category

- Each form's page, such as [I-140](/uscis-processing-times/i-140), now lists what USCIS's own processing-times page posts for each category of that form. That's the time within which 8 cases in 10 were decided, and the receipt day before which USCIS takes a question about a case.
- Where several offices post the same category, the row gives the quickest and the slowest.
- These pages refresh the day a new posting is copied.

## A deadline that lands on a weekend or holiday

- The [I-140 deadline guide](/guides/eta-9089-expiration-and-i140-deadline) now says what USCIS does when a certification's last day falls on a Saturday, a Sunday or a federal holiday. A paper petition is on time the next business day. One filed online gets no extra day.
- The [rules roundup](/blog/rule-changes-since-august-2026) names the policy alert of September 23, 2026 that set this. It took effect on October 23, 2026.

## Find a case from whatever you know

- The finder under every case number box now takes a range of dates, the job title and the law firm as well as the employer. [Open it](/perm-case-status), fill in what you know, and one box is enough. Before, it needed the employer and the exact day.
- One date looks at that day. Two dates look at every day between them, up to three months apart.
- A search by law firm lists decided cases only, because DOL names the firm at the decision. The finder says so when you use it.
- It works the same for PERM cases, wage requests, H-1B LCAs and the seasonal programs.
- The same search is in the [API](/developers) (`GET /v1/cases`), the MCP server (`find_case`) and the chat assistant.

## Easier on a phone

- On a phone, the strip of months on a [case's page](/perm-case-status) now fits the screen. "DOL is here" and your own month are both in view. Before, your month could sit off the right edge.
- The Data menu's handle now scrolls away with the top of the page. It used to float over the text as you read.
- Long names in the Data menu wrap onto a second line. Two of them were cut off.

## The case number box

- The box now shows words where it used to show a sample case number. The sample looked like a number already typed.
- Press the button with nothing typed and you stay in the box, with a line saying what to type.
- An address copied with a full stop or a bracket stuck to its end now opens the page it meant.

## Privacy: what we fixed

We read the Privacy Policy against the code, line by line, and fixed each place where the product did less than the policy says.

- A message sent through the contact form and held by the spam filter is now deleted after 30 days in both places it's stored. One copy used to stay.
- Deleting your account now also takes your address out of our email event log.
- A sign-up for the weekly digest or product news that nobody confirms is deleted after 30 days, like the other alerts.
- A USCIS receipt number in a page's address is stripped before our analytics sees it, the way a case number already was.
- The two analytics events our server sends now obey your account's analytics switch and your browser's Global Privacy Control signal.
- Session replay can't start while the policy says it's off.
- Your account is identified to our analytics by its number alone. Your name is no longer sent.

## Case tracking for legal teams

- A note whose save fails now stays in its box, so you can try again. It used to vanish with only a brief error message.
- Deleting a case now deletes the files uploaded to it as well. Every delete box says the same true thing: the case, its notes and its documents are erased, and it can't be undone. Two of those boxes used to say an administrator could undo it.
- Reopening several cases puts each one back at the stage its dates support. The box used to say they'd all return to the PWD stage.
- The job description card calls its figure "Prevailing wage". It was labelled "Wage offered", which is a different figure on Form 9089.
- A card moved to the top of a custom order now stays first.
- In Settings, the weekly summary and "Watch saved cases on DOL" switches show what you saved. They used to read "on" after every reload.

## Reminders and alerts

- Quiet hours no longer stop the daily deadline email. That email goes once a day, so one stopped by quiet hours was never sent. Quiet hours now hold browser push only, and urgent ones still come through.
- Browser push follows its own switch. With email off and push on, the push still arrives.
- "Manage notification settings" in an email now opens the Notifications tab. The test email's button used to open an address with no page.
- A queue alert for a wage request now names the wage queue on its confirmation pages. They used to say "PERM queue".
- The visa bulletin alert prints its month in words, and the date in an account deletion email is the Eastern day.

## What we say about ourselves

- Six of our own pages described the site more generously than it works: how often DOL is asked, how far back the bulletins go, what's encrypted and how sign-in works. Each now says what the site does, and the [corrections log](/changelog/corrections) lists them.
- Asking for a second sign-up code no longer fails on the security check. The form now waits for a fresh check before it lets you try again.

## Filings that point at each other

- A few H-1B LCAs name the wage request their wage came from. [Look one up](/perm-case-status) and its page now prints that request in DOL's record, linked to the request's own page.

## Figures that hold up when something breaks

- A page whose main figures can't be read keeps its last good version. A smaller figure that can't be read is left out. Neither is ever shown as zero or as "none".
- Right after each release, the stored figures behind the PERM pages are rebuilt. Until that finishes, a page says a count isn't ready yet, so new wording never sits on figures built by an older rule.
- The H-1B employer rankings in the [API](/developers) answer 503 when they can't be read, so a program can tell an outage from an empty result.
