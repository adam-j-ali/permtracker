People waiting on an I-485 copy the data their USCIS account shows and ask what codes like FTA0, IAF or IK1 mean. The new [USCIS account reader](/uscis-account-reader) lays it out for them.

## What it does

- Paste the case data your account sent, or lines that each hold a date and a code.
- It lists each update by date, with a few words for its code.
- Below the list, each code gets one explanation, with where that reading comes from. It's either USCIS's own code list from 2008, what filers consistently report, or no settled reading at all.
- A receipt number shows only as its letters and last four digits, and the letters name the service center.

## It stays in your browser

The reader never signs in to USCIS and never asks for your login. It reads your paste on the page, sends it nowhere, and analytics never records it.

## From your account to your timeline

Press "Add these to my timeline" and the updates fill the [green card timeline](/green-card-timelines) form on the same page, with the service center and the day USCIS received your I-485. Nothing is sent until you press Save, and a receipt number is never sent.

USCIS publishes no meanings for these codes, and a code is never a decision. USCIS decides a case in a notice.
