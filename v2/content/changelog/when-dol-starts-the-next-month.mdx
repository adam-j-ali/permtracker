People waiting on a PERM case ask the same thing every day: when will DOL start the next filing month? The [queue page](/perm-queue#next-month) now answers it from DOL's own record.

## When DOL starts the next month

- DOL starts a new filing month before it finishes the last one. Each of the last three times, the month before was about 80% to 82% decided, counting only the cases DOL can decide. Cases on hold are left out.
- The new section draws the month DOL is working down as one bar, with a band where the last three months began. Under the heading are the likely day, how many more decisions it takes and the pace DOL has kept since the month began.
- It lists the last three starts, with the day each began and how far the month before had got.
- A filing month's own page says when DOL will likely start it once it's the next one up. [March 2026](/perm-queue/2026-03) has it now.
- We ran the estimate on the record as it stood each working day. In the two weeks before each of the last two starts, it named the real day or a working day either side of it. Weekends and federal holidays don't count as working days.
