Until today, the figures on the [green card timelines](/green-card-timelines) page came only from the timelines shared on this site, so most of them said there weren't enough reports yet. They now count every case people report about themselves that we read: public Telegram groups about PERM and green cards, public community sheets, Reddit, VisaStats' tracker and the timelines shared here. Each person counts once. On Nov 20, 2026 that was 17,496 cases.

## What changed

- Each step's days come from every source: biometrics, the work permit, the transfer to a field office, the interview, the green card and the card in the mail, plus PERM, the I-140 and the consulate steps.
- Each step counts only the cases that finished it in the last 6 months, or 12 when 6 hold too few, so cases from past years don't outvote this year's.
- Each step says how it compares with the 6 months before, and a line shows the median month by month.
- Only the people heard from in the last year count as waiting, at the furthest step each reported.
- Each USCIS field office's page, the I-140, I-485, I-765, I-131 and I-129 processing time pages, the PERM processing times page and the consulate pages show the same figures for their own steps.

A timeline shared here privately counts in every figure, as the form promises, and is never named as a source.

A report that names the USCIS field office handling its case now counts toward that office's figures. Until today only the posts we read by hand carried an office. Reports about marriage and family cases are kept off the field office figures, which are about employment-based green cards.

## In your account

- Each case's I-140 tab now shows how I-140s like it are going: the median days from filing to a decision for its category, with or without premium processing, the share approved, and your case's own days beside it.
- A new switch in Settings counts your cases' dates in these figures. It's off unless you switch it on. No name, employer or case number is shared, and no case is ever listed or shown on its own.
