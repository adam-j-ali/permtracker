## What’s new

### Timeline view

The Gantt chart now draws your whole PERM timeline, with every date range and milestone:

- **Range bars** for PWD Validity, NOF Period, Filing Window, and ETA 9089 Certification
- **Milestone markers** for Audit Selected, I-140 Receipt, and I-140 Denied
- **Stacked rows** so overlapping ranges are readable at a glance
- **Dashed borders** distinguish calculated dates from dates you entered

Each range bar is color-coded and labeled inline, so you can scan across cases without hovering or clicking.

### Smarter AI chat

The AI assistant now uses a 5-model fallback chain that automatically switches providers when one is unavailable. Conversations are more reliable, and context stays intact across longer threads. You’ll see fewer interruptions during peak usage.

## Improved

### Redesigned homepage

The landing page has been rewritten with clearer messaging about what PERM Tracker actually does and why deadlines matter. It has less marketing fluff and more substance.

### Cleaner interface

A proportional scale-down across the app reduces visual clutter without removing any functionality. Cards, spacing, and typography are tighter and more balanced, especially on smaller screens.

### Better deadline hints

Recruitment deadline tooltips now explain *why* a date matters. Each hint includes the regulatory basis so you can verify the logic yourself.

### Range bar labels

Timeline range bars now always show their labels instead of only on hover, so you can see which bar is which date range.

## Fixed

- **Filter bar** now appears when all cases are archived. It previously showed the new-user empty state by mistake
- **"Clear All Filters"** correctly persists the cleared state across page navigation
- **Unknown routes** now show a proper 404 page instead of redirecting to login
- **Deadline enforcement** runs daily to close expired deadlines automatically, so stale cases no longer stay open
