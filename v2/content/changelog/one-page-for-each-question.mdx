We went through every public page and asked which question each one answers. Where two pages answered the same question, one now says its own job, or the two became one page.

## What changed

- [Today's decisions](/perm-updates), how many PERM cases DOL decided today, is in the header and second in the phone menu. The search box finds it from "decided today" too.
- The salary explorer's filters joined [PERM wages by job](/perm-wages#explore): pick an occupation, a worksite state, a fiscal year or an outcome there. The ladders for every state and the explorer's questions moved with them, and the old address opens the same filters.
- Pages renamed for their own job: [Browse decided PERM cases](/perm-cases), [Find a PERM law firm](/perm-attorneys), [Live PERM data](/tools), [PERM decisions over time](/perm-decision-activity), [Reported green card timelines](/green-card-timelines), [EB-2 vs EB-3](/tools/eb2-vs-eb3) and [where every green card step stands today](/today).
- The [PERM timeline calculator](/tools/perm-timeline-calculator) opens with what it does: an estimate for your filing date.
- On a page with an "On this page" bar, jumping to a part no longer tucks its heading under the bar.
- The header keeps every link from 1024px wide, and its colour reaches up behind it in apps' own browsers.
- On employer pages, a small state's map tile is named by the words it shows, so voice control can reach it.

## Corrected

- The [layoff notices](/layoffs) page said DOL's PERM files record no layoff. They record each employer's yes or no on a layoff in the job's occupation in the six months before filing, which [layoffs reported on PERM](/perm-layoff-disclosures) shows. The page now says so. The details are in [corrections](/changelog/corrections).
