## PERM Tracker 2.0 is here

PERM Tracker 2.0 is a complete rebuild of the original PERM case tracking tool, after months of development. It now has real-time data, automated calculations and a modern interface designed for immigration attorneys.

## What’s new in 2.0

### Real-time case tracking

Every change syncs instantly across devices, so there’s no stale data and no refresh button to press. Your case status is always current.

### Automated deadline engine

PERM Tracker works out each deadline under 20 CFR 656:

- PWD expiration dates
- Recruitment activity windows
- ETA 9089 filing windows
- I-140 180-day filing deadlines

Enter the main dates, and it does the math.

### Cascade updates

When you change a date, such as the PWD determination date, every deadline that depends on it recalculates at once.

### Recruitment tracking

A built-in checklist tracks all required recruitment activities with date validation, timing checks, and compliance verification.

### Calendar and timeline views

Two views for managing your caseload:

- **Calendar**: All deadlines in a familiar monthly/weekly format
- **Timeline**: Gantt-chart style view across multiple cases

### Multi-channel notifications

Deadline reminders come by:

- Email alerts
- Browser push notifications
- Dashboard urgency indicators

### AI assistant

Ask about PERM regulations, get guidance on a specific case and explore the knowledge base, all inside the app.

### Progressive web app

Install PERM Tracker as a native app on your phone or desktop. It works offline for viewing and syncs when connected.

## What’s coming

Here’s what’s on the roadmap:

- **Content hub**: Blog, tutorials, and guides for PERM practitioners
- **Bulk operations**: Manage multiple cases simultaneously
- **Advanced analytics**: Processing time trends, audit risk indicators
- **Team features**: Multi-attorney case collaboration
