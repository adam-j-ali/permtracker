People waiting on an I-485 watch their USCIS account for silent updates and ask what a code like FTA0 or IK1 means. The [green card timelines](/green-card-timelines) form now records them, so the people waiting after you can see what others saw.

## What you can add

- Each update your account showed: the day, the form it was on, and the code. Pick the code from the ones filers name most, type it under Other if yours isn't listed, or leave it out when only a date moved.
- The service center that took in your I-485. The first three letters of your receipt number name it.
- The day your Supplement J (I-485J) was approved.

Like every other date on the form, each one is optional, and you don't need an account or a case number.

## What others see

- Once five timelines report a code, the timelines page and the office's page show how many people saw it.
- A shared row shows how many updates it has and their codes, never their dates.
- A service center is named on a row once three shared timelines name it.

## Checked before it shows

Every timeline waits for a review before it counts. A typed code is the one thing on the form that isn't picked from a list, so we read it first. An edit that adds a new typed code puts the timeline back in line. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) says what's kept and what's shown.

The form is on [the green card timelines page](/green-card-timelines), on each [USCIS office's page](/i485-by-field-office), and on a PERM case's page.
