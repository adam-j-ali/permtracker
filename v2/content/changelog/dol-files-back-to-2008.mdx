Until this week, most pages read DOL's PERM files from fiscal year 2024 on, and its H-1B LCA files from FY2020. They now read every file DOL has published, PERM from FY2008 and LCAs from FY2008, so an older case, employer or firm has its whole record.

## What changed

- The [case search](/case-search) finds PERM decisions from FY2008 to FY2023 too, and each state's count adds them.
- An older PERM case's page shows its own wage request (the number, the prevailing wage, its level and its dates) and where its wage sits among its own year's cases.
- Employer and firm pages show what their older cases were made of, each figure labelled with the years it covers. An employer with no PERM page now shows its H-1B LCAs year by year.
- [Decision activity](/perm-decision-activity) answers for LCAs decided from FY2008 to FY2019 from DOL's older files.
- City pages count the older LCA files, and occupation pages show who filed each job and where from FY2008.
- The [salary explorer](/perm-wages#explore) and the denial rate by wage band reach back to FY2008, and the [PERM wait trend](/perm-processing-times) reaches back to FY2015.
- A sponsor's matching against Wage and Hour and OSHA records covers its older PERM worksites too.
- The case search's review-stage filter now lists the H-2A, H-2B and CW-1 stages.
- A seasonal case links its job, employer, law firm, job order and wage determination, and a wage request's page links the LCAs and job orders that cite it.

## Also this week

- The [immigrant visa interview pause](/guides/immigrant-visa-interview-pause) guide covers the plaintiffs' November 23 motion in *CLINIC v. Rubio*, read from the court's own docket. It sets out the procedures the motion challenges (the IVAR review among them), what it asks the court to order and the proposed schedule.
- The [green card timelines](/green-card-timelines) read people's reports from WhatsApp group chats too, exported by hand. A work permit's or travel document's approval is never counted as the green card's.
- The [USCIS case status](/uscis-case-status) page now says what a receipt number's digits mean. On a paper filing they're the fiscal year, the day it arrived and its place in that day's count, while a 9 after the year marks a lockbox's running count for the whole year. An online number counts up in one run and carries neither. The page used to show a "day" no year has.
- [Editorial standards](/editorial-standards) and [About](/about) now say that some figures come from what people report about their own cases, and that those pages say so.
- A new note reads [DOL's PERM file for July to September 2026](/blog/dol-perm-file-fy2026-q4): how many cases DOL decided, how long they took, and who filed the most.
- The [EB-3 Other Workers](/guides/eb3-other-workers) and [EB-2 India](/guides/approved-i140-no-visa-number-eb2-india) guides now show the newest bulletin's dates, read from the same archive as the [visa bulletin page](/visa-bulletin), instead of a month typed in by hand.
- The [green card timelines](/green-card-timelines), [today](/today) and the [estimate scorecard](/estimate-scorecard) pages now end with a line naming where their figures come from and how current each source is.
