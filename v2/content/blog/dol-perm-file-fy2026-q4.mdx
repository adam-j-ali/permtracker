## What stands out this quarter?

DOL decided about an eighth more PERM cases than in April to June, and more than a quarter more than a year before. The cases it certified had waited more than three months less. Five things stand out, all from DOL's files:

- The median certified case took more than three months less from filing than in April to June, and over four months less than a year before.
- DOL certified about the same share of what it decided as in April to June, a little less than a year before, and its denials were about three quarters more than a year before.
- Three Amazon companies (Amazon Dev Center U.S., Amazon Web Services and Amazon.com Services) went from a few dozen decided cases each in April to June to hundreds each. NVIDIA's decided cases doubled again, to more than six times a year before.
- The median wage on certified cases rose again, by about a fifteenth on April to June and about a sixth on a year before.
- DOL certified about half as many H-1B LCAs as in April to June, when the lottery's winners file, and about as many as a year before. It determined about a fifth more wage requests than in April to June, though fewer than a year before.

## What this file can't tell you

The file holds decided cases only, so it says nothing about the cases still waiting. The [PERM queue](/perm-queue) counts those from DOL's live record, by the month they were filed.

It counts each case on the day DOL decided it. A busy quarter here may be DOL working through an older filing month. The wait figures say how old the decided cases were.

DOL doesn't say why it denied a case. A withdrawal is the employer's own choice. An employer that files under several names is counted once, on its own page, and a spelling of a name that this site can't tie to any page is counted apart from it.

## Where the figures come from

Every figure is from DOL's quarterly PERM, prevailing wage and LCA disclosure files, counted by the day DOL decided each case. The [PERM case search](/case-search) reads the same files one case at a time, and each employer and law firm links to its full record.
