You’ll learn how to use the automated deadline engine in PERM Tracker's free [case-management app](/for-attorneys) to calculate PWD expiration dates, recruitment filing windows and I-140 deadlines. The app also syncs each case's deadlines to Google Calendar. This tutorial covers email and push notifications and the calendar and timeline views too, so no PERM date slips past you.

## Why deadline tracking matters

In PERM practice, missing a single deadline can mean:

- An expired PWD means starting the prevailing wage process over and waiting again (<DolNow show="pwdWait" />) per [20 CFR 656.40](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/chapter-V/part-656/subpart-D/section-656.40)
- A missed filing window can mean redoing recruitment under the [180-day rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/chapter-V/part-656/subpart-C/section-656.17)
- An expired certification can't be filed: the 180-day I-140 filing window has no extension
- Clients rely on their attorney to manage the timelines, so a missed one is a malpractice risk

<Callout type="stat" stat="50+ dates" title="Dates to track in one case">
  A single PERM case generates 50+ interconnected dates and deadlines across its lifecycle. At 10+ active cases, that’s hundreds of deadlines to track, more than manual methods can keep up with.
</Callout>

When you’re managing 10, 20, or 50+ active cases, manual tracking with spreadsheets becomes a liability. For a detailed comparison of approaches, see [manual vs. automated tracking](/guides/manual-vs-automated-tracking).

## How PERM Tracker calculates deadlines

PERM Tracker uses a rules engine based on [20 CFR 656](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/chapter-V/part-656) to automatically calculate every deadline from the dates you enter. Here’s how it works:

### Cascade system

When you enter or update a key date, the system automatically recalculates all dependent deadlines. For example:

1. Enter the **PWD determination date**, and the app works out when the determination expires (90 days, or the next June 30, depending on the date)
2. Enter the **first recruitment activity date**, and the app works out the 180-day filing window
3. Enter the **PERM certification date**, and the app works out the I-140 deadline (certification + 179 days, the date DOL prints)

With this cascade, you never calculate a deadline by hand. You just enter dates as they happen.

### Validation warnings

Beyond deadline calculation, PERM Tracker validates your case data against PERM regulations:

- It warns when a PWD is about to expire or has expired.
- It flags required recruitment activities that are missing.
- It shows whether you’re within the valid filing window.
- It points out cases whose deadlines overlap or conflict.

## Setting up notifications

PERM Tracker offers three notification channels:

### Email notifications

Configure email alerts to receive deadline reminders at your preferred advance notice period (7 days, 14 days, 30 days before).

### Push notifications

Enable browser push notifications so deadline alerts reach you even when you’re not in PERM Tracker. They go out on a schedule, so they won’t arrive the instant a date turns.

### Dashboard indicators

The Dashboard uses color-coded urgency indicators:

- **Green**: Plenty of time remaining
- **Blue**: Approaching (weeks away)
- **Orange**: Soon (days away)
- **Red**: Urgent or overdue

## Timeline view

For a big-picture view, the Timeline shows all your cases on a Gantt-chart style timeline. Each case is represented as a horizontal bar, with key milestones marked along the way.

This view helps you:

- See where each case stands relative to others
- Identify bottlenecks across your caseload
- Plan staffing and resource allocation

## Four habits that keep deadlines from slipping

Enter dates right away, review the calendar weekly, turn on more than one notification channel, and check the dashboard daily.

1. Enter dates as soon as they happen, rather than catching up later.
2. Review the Calendar weekly, so upcoming deadlines show up before they’re urgent.
3. Turn on email and push notifications both, so one missed alert isn’t the only one.
4. Check the Dashboard daily: its urgency indicators show what to do first.
