# Terms of Service lines for the USCIS receipt alerts beta (draft)

Nothing here is on the site. Adding any of it to the Terms is the owner's call. Each line says where it would go and
why it's suggested.

## 2. Description of Service

After "and subscribe to email alerts", one sentence:

> Receipt alerts for USCIS cases are a beta: we may change how often we check, pause them, or end them, and we'll
> say so by email first when we end them.

Why: the beta checks every 4 hours and holds 2,000 receipts at most. The Terms shouldn't promise more than that.

## 4. Acceptable Use

One new item in the list:

> Sign up only a receipt number that is yours, or one whose owner has asked you to watch it for them.

Why: the lookup page already asks for this when a receipt is typed. A watch keeps the number for months, so the
same rule should cover it.

## 7. Disclaimers

One sentence, after the paragraph on government data:

> A receipt alert reports the status USCIS showed when we checked, and the time we checked. USCIS is the authority
> on every case. An alert can arrive late or not at all, and its absence never means nothing has changed.

Why: the checker can be offline for a while. Every email already says when we checked, and this says the same about
an email that doesn't come.

## 12. Termination

One sentence:

> We may stop receipt alerts for an address that we have reason to think is watching receipts that aren't its own.

Why: the per-address cap and the double opt-in bound the abuse, and this names the remedy if both are worked around.
